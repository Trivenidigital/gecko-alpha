# F4 — young token AND volume uptrend (long-hold study, 2026-09-18)

**Prereg:** `prereg_long_hold_v2.md`, SHA-256 `f7d38fa219359f6a82d2a4d1920509fabff7627ae1529ce6e5c1318e952988a5`
(short hash `f7d38fa2`), written 2026-09-18 before the v2 feature data was
collected. It inherits universe, entry, exit, benchmark and the bad-chart
exclusion from v1 (`prereg_long_hold.md`, hash `cdb14c95`).

## Rule

- **F1 young token:** `token_age_days <= 90`, where `token_age_days = t0 -
  pool_created_at` of the coin's highest-liquidity pool on the first CoinGecko
  platform GeckoTerminal resolves.
- **F3 volume uptrend:** `vol_ratio >= 2.0`, where `vol_ratio` = DEX volume in
  the 7 daily candles before t0 / volume in the 7 candles before those (same
  pool); undefined when the earlier window is 0.
- **F4 = F1 AND F3.** Thresholds live in `GeckoSettings.f4_max_age_days` (90)
  and `GeckoSettings.f4_min_vol_ratio` (2.0).

Coins with no resolvable pool are feature-missing: excluded from F1/F3/F4,
never treated as pass or fail.

## Universe

Every CoinGecko `coin_id` flagged by any surface with a recorded price
(predictions, paper_trades, velocity_alerts, slow_burn_candidates,
gainer_acceleration, momentum_7d, volume_spikes), 2026-04-09 .. 2026-09-18.

- Entry = first flag timestamp t0; entry price and mcap re-read from CoinGecko
  `market_chart` at the point nearest t0 (dead if > 36 h away or price <= 0).
- Exit = hold to 2026-09-18 (last chart point). Multiple `x = last / p0`.
- Benchmark = BTC held over the same window per coin, equal weight.
- Exclusion: coins whose chart entry price / earliest stored flag price is
  outside [0.5, 2.0] (bad or re-denominated charts). Coins with no stored
  price are kept (unverifiable).

## Pass criteria (all required)

1. Mean multiple > 1.0 AND > the BTC mean for the same coins.
2. Mean excluding the single best coin > BTC mean.
3. Mean > the all-flagged (R0) mean for coins first flagged in the same months.
4. Both halves split at 2026-07-01 beat BTC; if a half has n < 20, split the
   rule's own coins at their median t0 instead.
5. Basket size between 20 and 200.

## Fragility caveats

F4 passed, but the pass is thin and tail-driven:

- **Mean ex-top-3 is 0.75x.** Without its three best coins the basket loses
  money and trails BTC (1.07x).
- **Median is 0.53x.** The typical F4 coin roughly halves.
- **49% of coins are at or below 0.5x.**
- **Bootstrap 90% CI on the mean is 0.88x to 3.19x**, which includes BTC.
- **P(mean <= BTC) is about 0.10** under the bootstrap.

The bootstrap figures come from the original 2026-09-18 analysis; the harness
does not recompute them. No pass authorizes live money: a forward paper test is
required.

## Live versus study vol_ratio definition

**The live rule equals the registered definition.** Both the harness and the
live ranker (`gecko.features.vol_ratio_from_candles`) compute
`last = sum(vol, [t0-7d, t0))` over `prior = sum(vol, [t0-14d, t0-7d))`, with
no minimum candle count. **Any stricter minimum requires a new prereg.**

The live function adds only input hardening that does not change the registered
quantity on clean data. It drops malformed candles and collapses duplicate
timestamps to the last one. It returns None when the prior week sums to zero or
the last week has no candle; the study yields None or 0.0 there, and both fail
F3. On the study data the two definitions agree on F4 for every coin: the
divergence is 0.

History: the first live implementation also required at least 14 usable candles
in the window. That minimum was never part of prereg `f7d38fa2`. On the study
data it disagreed with the registered rule on 34 coins. All 34 passed F4 and
had pools with fewer than 14 daily candles before t0. They averaged 2.47x, and
the basket without them would have been 67 coins at 1.63x. It silently dropped
the youngest third of the cohort, so it was removed on 2026-10-09 by ruling.
